## Evidence collected

I found published measurements for some instruction-following benchmarks. I did **not** find published measurements for Q4_K_M/Q5_K_M/Q6_K/Q8_0 specifically on the requested properties.

# 1. Published work measuring quantised models on instruction-following benchmarks

## QUOTED

**Paper:** *A Comprehensive Evaluation of Quantized Instruction-Tuned Large Language Models: An Experimental Analysis up to 405B* (2024)

Link: ([arXiv][1])

> “This paper evaluates the performance of instruction-tuned LLMs across various quantization methods (GPTQ, AWQ, SmoothQuant, and FP8) on models ranging from 7B to 405B.”

> “Using 13 benchmarks, we assess various task types: commonsense Q&A, knowledge and language understanding, instruction following, hallucination detection, mathematics, and dialogue.”

([arXiv][1])

**INFERRED**

This is a direct match for instruction-following evaluation. It does not evaluate llama.cpp GGUF levels such as Q4_K_M.

---

## QUOTED

**Paper:** *QLoRA: Efficient Finetuning of Quantized LLMs* (2023)

Link: ([arXiv][2])

> “We use QLoRA to finetune more than 1,000 models, providing a detailed analysis of instruction following and chatbot performance across 8 instruction datasets…”

([arXiv][2])

**INFERRED**

QLoRA studies 4-bit quantisation, but it is a different setup from llama.cpp GGUF inference quants.

# 2. Does quantisation degrade instruction-following / format adherence / stopping more than general capability?

## QUOTED

The 2024 evaluation reports:

> “performance varies significantly with different quantization methods, model size, and bit-width…”

and includes instruction-following among evaluated task types. ([arXiv][1])

Qwen’s published quantisation table reports IFEval (Strict Prompt-Level Accuracy):

| Model               | BF16 | GPTQ-Int4 |  AWQ |
| ------------------- | ---: | --------: | ---: |
| Qwen2-7B-Instruct   | 53.1 |      49.4 | 51.4 |
| Qwen2-1.5B-Instruct | 29.0 |      27.0 | 29.9 |
| Qwen2-72B-Instruct  | 77.6 |      78.9 | 76.9 |

Source: ([Qwen][3])

**INFERRED**

There is evidence that some 4-bit methods reduce instruction-following benchmark scores.

**NOT FOUND**

I did not find published measurements showing:

* failure to stop generation,
* two-hour reasoning runs with no answer,
* exact format-template completion failures,
* structured-output adherence compared directly against perplexity loss.

# 4. Quantisation effects on reasoning models, especially termination failures

## QUOTED

A 2025 long-context quantisation study states:

> “We present the first systematic evaluation of quantized LLMs on tasks with long-inputs (>64K tokens) and long-form outputs.”

It evaluated:

> “five quantization methods … and five models.”

([arXiv][4])

**INFERRED**

There is some evidence that quantisation interacts with difficult tasks, especially long-context tasks.

**NOT FOUND**

I did not find published measurements specifically on:

* reasoning-model chain-of-thought termination,
* failure to end reasoning,
* quantisation causing runaway internal reasoning.
