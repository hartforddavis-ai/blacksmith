# 1. Benchmarks measuring instruction-following / format adherence

## IFEval (Instruction-Following Evaluation)

**QUOTED**

> “IFEval measures LLM ability to follow verifiable formatting instructions (e.g., output length, keyword inclusion, capitalization) with exact-match checking.” ([LLM Reference][1])

**Evidence**

IFEval tests explicit, objectively checkable constraints such as:

* output length,
* required keywords,
* structure,
* formatting constraints. ([EvalScope][2])

Link:
([DeepEval][3])

**QUOTED**

> “FollowEval benchmark ... assess[es] LLMs across five critical dimensions of instruction following: string manipulation, commonsense reasoning, logical reasoning, spatial reasoning, and response constraints.” ([arXiv][4])

**NOT FOUND**

**QUOTED**
