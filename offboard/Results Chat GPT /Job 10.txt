# Final question

> Does published evidence support or contradict the idea that a sub-15B model can do this reliably?

**Evidence-supported answer:**

The published evidence **does not establish reliable performance** for that exact task.

What is supported:

* Models can produce unsupported claims even when given source material. ([arXiv][5])
* Attribution/grounding is a recognized failure area requiring dedicated evaluation. ([Google Research][1])
* Smaller open models show substantial hallucination counts in source-grounded generation benchmarks. ([GitHub][4])

What is **not established**:

* that a 9–12B model cannot verify verbatim quotes reliably;
* the exact failure rate for that task.

Final classification:

**UNVERIFIED** — “a sub-15B model cannot reliably verify quotes.”

**SUPPORTED** — “source-grounded verification is a known failure area for language models, and reliability must be measured rather than assumed.” ([Google Research][1])
