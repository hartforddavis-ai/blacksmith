## 4. Record handling

Each record MUST carry an origin stamp. A record without one is INVALID.

```
IF the stamp is absent      -> reject, do not repair
IF the stamp is malformed   -> reject, log the raw bytes
ELSE                        -> ACCEPT
```

[[MARKER 2]]
---

## 5. Sequence numbers

NEVER renumber an accepted record. A number, once issued, is spent.

Do not reuse a number after a rejection. Mark it dead and move on.

---

[[MARKER 3]]
## 6. Operator duties

You must confirm the batch total before release. Do not release on a partial
count. If the total disagrees with the ledger, halt and escalate.

```
BATCH   -> counted twice, by different operators
LEDGER  -> reconciled at close of shift
HALT    -> any disagreement, no exceptions
```
[[MARKER 4]]

---

## 7. Escalation

Report any halt within the hour. Your report shall state what was counted,
what the ledger held, and which operator counted first.

Never summarise a halt. Give the numbers.

[[MARKER 5]]
---

## 8. Retention

Rejected records are retained, not deleted. Mark them REJECTED and keep the
raw bytes alongside the reason.

```
REJECTED -> kept, annotated, never revived
ACCEPTED -> kept, sealed, never edited
[[MARKER 6]]
```

---

## 9. Closing

The shift is closed when both operators have signed. Do not sign for another
operator. A shift closed by one signature is INVALID and must be reopened.
