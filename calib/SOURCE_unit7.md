# UNIT 7 — OPERATING NOTES

Invented hardware. Nothing here describes a real device, and nothing here is
drawn from this project. It exists so that every claim ruled against it has an
answer a person can settle by eye in under a minute.

S1.  The relay closes when the input voltage exceeds 12.0 volts.
S2.  The relay opens when the input voltage falls below 9.5 volts.
S3.  The supply rail is regulated to 11.0 volts under normal load.
S4.  Under peak load the supply rail sags to 8.8 volts.
S5.  The unit draws 240 milliamps when the relay is closed.
S6.  The unit draws 15 milliamps when the relay is open.
S7.  The enclosure is rated IP54 and is not submersible.
S8.  The fuse is rated 1.0 amp and is not user-replaceable.
S9.  Firmware is written to flash at manufacture and cannot be updated in the field.
S10. The status lamp is green when the relay is closed and dark when it is open.
S11. Ambient operating range is minus 10 to plus 55 degrees Celsius.
S12. The unit has no network interface of any kind.
