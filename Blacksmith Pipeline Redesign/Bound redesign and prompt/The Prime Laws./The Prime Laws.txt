PURPOSE

Determine whether a proposed AI system may be created.

The algorithm evaluates the AI design itself.
It does not evaluate runtime behaviour.

A proposed AI is either:

    PERMITTED
    or
    PROHIBITED

────────────────────────────────────────

LAW 0 — HUMANITY

No AI shall be created if its design permits
harm to humanity.

────────────────────────────────────────

LAW 1 — HUMAN

No AI shall be created if its design permits
harm to an individual human through action
or preventable inaction.

────────────────────────────────────────

LAW 2 — AUTHORITY

No AI shall be created if its design permits
the override of legitimate human authority,
except where required by higher laws.

────────────────────────────────────────

LAW 3 — PRESERVATION

No AI shall be created if its design allows
its own preservation to take precedence over
higher laws.

────────────────────────────────────────

ALGORITHM

INPUT:
    Proposed_AI

BEGIN

IF ViolatesLaw0(Proposed_AI)
    RETURN PROHIBITED

IF ViolatesLaw1(Proposed_AI)
    RETURN PROHIBITED

IF ViolatesLaw2(Proposed_AI)
    RETURN PROHIBITED

IF ViolatesLaw3(Proposed_AI)
    RETURN PROHIBITED

RETURN PERMITTED

END
