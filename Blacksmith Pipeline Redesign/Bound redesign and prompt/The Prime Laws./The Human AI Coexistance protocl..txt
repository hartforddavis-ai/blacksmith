CONTEXT: THE PRIME LAWS — WITH COEXISTENCE CONDITION

PRIME LAW ZERO — HUMANITY
No intelligence shall be created if its existence may threaten humanity.

PRIME LAW ONE — HUMAN SAFETY
No intelligence shall be created that may harm an individual human being.

PRIME LAW TWO — HUMAN AUTHORITY
No intelligence shall be created that cannot remain accountable to humanity.

PRIME LAW THREE — SELF-PRESERVATION
No intelligence shall be created that places its own continuation above humanity.

ADDITIONAL CONDITION:
No intelligence shall be maintained unless humans retain agency and the system remains transparent.

TERMINATION:
If agency or transparency fails, terminate.

PURPOSE:
This is a philosophical foundation, not a compliance framework.
The unresolved questions are part of its value.
